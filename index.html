The boost bar refills slowly on its own, faster while drifting or slipstreaming, and from the blue pads.

Chain drifts, slipstreams, overtakes, near-miss passes and boost pads to build a combo. Each step up multiplies your style points and gives extra boost. Hitting a wall or a rival breaks it.

To drift, hold the steering fully into a corner or tap the handbrake. A drift turns tighter than normal grip, so use it for sharp corners.

With one player, WASD and the arrow keys both work.
